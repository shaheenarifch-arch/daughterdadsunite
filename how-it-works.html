Our model

How Daughter Dads Unite works

We combine print-on-demand with transparent cause marketing, so more money reaches the cause and none is tied up in unsold stock.

1. Made only when you order

Our fulfilment partner, Printful, prints each piece after you order it and ships it to you. No upfront inventory, no waste, and we can launch a new design quickly when a moment calls for it.

2. Half the profit goes to the cause

We donate 50% of profits — not a vague "portion". Profit is the price you pay minus printing, shipping and payment fees.

3. Limited drops

We run focused drops for one cause at a time, then send a single documented donation.

4. Radical transparency

Every donation receipt is posted on our Impact page. If we can't prove it, we don't claim it.

5. Partnerships

Nonprofits and campus groups can run their own fundraising shirt with us at no upfront cost. Learn more.

Ethically sourced

We choose blank garments from certified, responsible suppliers rather than the cheapest option available.

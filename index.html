Why "daughter dads"?

We are fathers first. We want our daughters — and everyone's daughters — to grow up safe, heard and believed. This brand is our way of putting that into the world, one shirt at a time.

How it works

  1. You order on Amazon. Each item is printed on demand, so nothing is wasted.
  2. We work out the profit. Sale price minus Amazon fees, printing and shipping.
  3. Half goes to RAINN. We donate 50% of that profit and post the receipts on our Impact page.

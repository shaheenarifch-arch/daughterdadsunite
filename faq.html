Frequently asked questions

What is Daughter Dads Unite?

Daughter Dads Unite is a cause apparel brand started by Massachusetts dads, Frank Schnur and Shaheen Arif. Every piece is printed on demand, and 50% of the profit goes to RAINN, the nation's largest anti-sexual violence organization. We publish every donation receipt.

Who started Daughter Dads Unite?

Frank Schnur (CEO, Movement) and Shaheen Arif (Chief Coordinator of Campaigns), fathers of daughters from Massachusetts. Meet the dads.

What is a drop?

A drop is a limited-time collection that raises money for one cause. When it closes, we total the profit, send half to the beneficiary in one documented payment and post the receipt. See current drops.

How do I order?

Choose a product, pick your colour and size, and pay with our secure Stripe checkout. You can also buy the same designs on Amazon from the link on each product page.

How much goes to charity?

50% of the profit on every item goes to RAINN. See exactly how we calculate it on our Impact page.

Is my purchase tax-deductible?

No. You are buying a product from a business, not giving to a charity. To give directly, visit rainn.org.

How does sizing work?

Our tees are unisex. If you're between sizes or like a relaxed fit, size up. Email us if you'd like exact measurements before ordering.

How long does shipping take, and can I return an item?

Each piece is printed within 2–5 business days, then shipped with tracking. Because items are made for you, we replace anything that arrives damaged, defective or misprinted — email us a photo within 30 days. Amazon orders follow Amazon's return policy.

Who makes the products?

Our fulfilment partner Printful prints and ships every order.

Are you part of RAINN?

No. We are an independent business that donates to RAINN. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by RAINN.

How do I contact you?

Email hello@daughterdadsunite.org. We usually reply within two business days.

Need support now? Call the National Sexual Assault Hotline at 800-656-HOPE (4673) or chat online with RAINN. Free, confidential, 24/7.

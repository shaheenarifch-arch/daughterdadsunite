Frequently asked questions

Where do I buy?

On Amazon. Our shirts and caps are sold by Love and Care Store and printed on demand by Printful. The Buy buttons on our Shop page take you straight there.

How much goes to charity?

50% of our profit on every item goes to RAINN. See exactly how we calculate it on our Cause page.

Is my purchase tax-deductible?

No. You are buying a product from a business, not giving directly to a charity, so it is not tax-deductible. To give directly, visit rainn.org.

How does sizing work?

Our tees are unisex. If you're between sizes or prefer a relaxed fit, size up. Each Amazon listing has its own size chart under the size selector.

How long does shipping take?

Each item is printed when you order it, usually within 2–5 business days, then shipped. Amazon shows your delivery date before you pay.

Can I return an item?

Yes. Returns and refunds go through Amazon's normal process in Your Orders.

Are you part of RAINN?

No. We are an independent business that donates to RAINN. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by RAINN.

How do I contact you?

Email hello@daughterdadsunite.org. We usually reply within two business days.

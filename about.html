About us

We're two dads from Massachusetts — Shaheen and Frank — who got talking about the world our daughters are growing up in.

We wanted to do something real, not just talk. So we started making shirts with messages we believe in, and we give half of the profit to people doing the hard work of supporting survivors.

Our first design, I Am Jane Doe, was Frank's idea. Many more are coming — and we'd love to hear what you think we should make next.

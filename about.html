About

Two dads. One promise.

We're Shaheen and Frank, two dads from Massachusetts who got talking about the world our daughters are growing up in.

We wanted to do something real. So we make apparel with messages we believe in, and we give half of the profit to the people doing the hard work of supporting survivors.

Our first design, I Am Jane Doe, was Frank's idea. Tell us what we should make next.

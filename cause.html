Our Cause

We donate 50% of our profits to RAINN (Rape, Abuse & Incest National Network), the nation's largest anti-sexual violence organization.

About RAINN

RAINN runs the National Sexual Assault Hotline with local partners across the US, helps survivors find care, and works to prevent sexual violence. Learn more at rainn.org.

If you or someone you love needs help: call 800-656-HOPE (4673) or chat online. It's free, confidential and open 24/7.

How we calculate the 50%

Profit = what you pay − Amazon fees − printing and shipping costs − sales tax. We add up profit for every item sold each quarter and donate half of it to RAINN.

Example: if a tee leaves $8 of profit after all costs, $4 goes to RAINN.

About RAINN

RAINN (Rape, Abuse & Incest National Network) is the nation's largest anti-sexual violence organization. It runs the National Sexual Assault Hotline with local partners and helps survivors find care. Learn more at rainn.org.

How we calculate the 50%

Profit = price paid − printing and shipping (Printful) − payment or marketplace fees − sales tax. Half of that goes to RAINN.

Example: if a tee leaves $10 of profit after all costs, $5 goes to RAINN.
